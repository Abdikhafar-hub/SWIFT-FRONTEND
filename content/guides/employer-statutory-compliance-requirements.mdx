## Overview

Employers in Kenya have several statutory obligations that must be met monthly. Failure to comply can result in penalties, interest charges, and legal consequences. This guide covers the key employer obligations.

## NSSF (National Social Security Fund)

### Obligation

All employers must register with NSSF and make monthly contributions for their employees.

### Key Requirements

- Register as an employer with NSSF
- Register each employee
- Calculate contributions based on current statutory rates
- Deduct employee contributions from payroll
- Remit total contributions monthly

### Deadline

Monthly contributions must be remitted by the statutory deadline each month.

## SHA (Social Health Authority)

### Obligation

Employers must register with SHA and make monthly health insurance contributions for employees.

### Key Requirements

- Register as an employer with SHA
- Enroll employees and their eligible dependants
- Calculate contributions based on current rates
- Deduct and remit contributions monthly

### Deadline

Monthly contributions are due by the statutory deadline.

## PAYE (Pay As You Earn)

### Obligation

Employers must deduct income tax from employee salaries and remit to KRA.

### Key Requirements

- Register for PAYE with KRA
- Calculate tax deductions based on current tax tables
- Deduct tax from employee salaries
- File monthly PAYE returns on iTax
- Remit deductions to KRA

### Deadline

PAYE returns and payments are due by the 9th of the following month.

## Housing Levy

### Obligation

As mandated by current legislation, employers must deduct and remit housing levy contributions.

### Key Requirements

- Calculate contributions based on current rates
- Deduct from employee salaries
- Remit monthly alongside PAYE

## Monthly Compliance Summary

| Obligation | Deadline | Platform |
|---|---|---|

| NSSF | Statutory deadline | NSSF |
| SHA | Statutory deadline | SHA |
