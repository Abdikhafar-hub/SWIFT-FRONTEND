## Overview

This guide provides a detailed breakdown of the documents and information required for company registration in Kenya. Having all requirements ready before starting the process helps avoid delays.

## Identification Documents

### For Directors

- Valid National ID card (front and back) OR valid passport
- KRA PIN certificate (each director must have their own)
- Passport-size photograph (recent, clear photo)

### For Shareholders

- Valid National ID card or passport
- KRA PIN certificate
- For corporate shareholders: Certificate of Incorporation and board resolution

## Company Details Required

### Company Name

- Prepare at least three proposed company names in order of preference
- Names must not be identical or too similar to existing registered names
- Avoid restricted words that require special approval

### Registered Office

- Physical address in Kenya (not a P.O. Box)
- This is where official correspondence will be sent
- Can be changed later by filing the appropriate form with BRS

### Share Capital

- Minimum nominal share capital as required by the Companies Act
- Details of share allocation among shareholders
- Share classes (ordinary, preference) if applicable

## Constitutional Documents

### Memorandum of Association

- States the company name, registered office, and objects
- Declares the subscribers and their share allocation

### Articles of Association

- Governs the internal management of the company
- Covers director appointment, shareholder meetings, dividend policies
- Standard model articles can be adopted or customized

## Additional Requirements

### For Foreign Directors or Shareholders

- Valid passport (certified copy)
- Work permit or special pass if applicable
- Foreign address details

### For Specific Business Activities

- Some business activities may require additional regulatory approvals
- Professional firms may need relevant professional body approvals
