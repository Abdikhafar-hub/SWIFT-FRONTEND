## What You Need Before Applying

Before submitting a TCC application, ensure:

1. **All tax returns are filed** — Check your iTax account for any outstanding returns
2. **No pending tax assessments** — Resolve or respond to any assessments
3. **No outstanding tax liabilities** — Pay any pending taxes or set up an approved arrangement
4. **Your iTax profile is updated** — Current address, email, and phone number

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Step 1: Log Into iTax

Access your KRA iTax account. Ensure you are using the correct KRA PIN for the certificate you need (individual or business).

### Step 2: Navigate to TCC Application

Find the Tax Compliance Certificate section in the iTax portal and initiate a new application.

### Step 3: System Compliance Check

The iTax system automatically checks your compliance history. If issues are found, the application may be flagged and you will need to resolve them first.

### Step 4: Submit Application

If the compliance check passes, submit the application. Processing time varies.

### Step 5: Download Certificate

Once approved, download the TCC from your iTax account. Note the validity period.

## Tips for Successful Application

- **File all returns early** — Don't wait until the deadline
- **Keep records organized** — Maintain copies of all tax-related documents
- **Monitor your iTax account** — Check regularly for any KRA communications
- **Apply before you urgently need it** — Give yourself buffer time for processing
- **Address issues proactively** — If you know you have outstanding obligations, resolve them before applying

## What If Your Application Is Rejected?

If the TCC application is rejected:

1. Check the reason for rejection on iTax
2. Address the specific compliance gap identified
3. Reapply after resolving all issues
4. Contact Swift Doc for assistance with complex compliance issues
