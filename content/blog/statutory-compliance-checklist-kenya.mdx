## Why Compliance Matters

Statutory compliance is not optional for Kenyan businesses. Non-compliance can result in penalties, fines, loss of business permits, and legal consequences. Maintaining compliance also builds credibility with clients, partners, and regulatory bodies.

## Business Registration Compliance

### BRS (Business Registration Service)

- **Annual Returns** — Companies must file annual returns with BRS
- **Director Changes** — Any changes to directors or shareholders must be reported
- **Address Changes** — Registered office address changes must be updated
- **Business Name Renewal** — Business name registrations require periodic renewal

### County Business Permits

- Business permits and trade licences from the relevant county government
- Renewal deadlines vary by county

## Tax Compliance (KRA)

### Income Tax

- **Company Income Tax** — Annual filing and payment
- **Individual Income Tax** — For sole proprietors and partners
- **Advance Tax** — As applicable to certain business activities

### Value Added Tax (VAT)

- Monthly VAT returns for registered businesses
- VAT registration is mandatory when turnover exceeds the threshold

### Pay As You Earn (PAYE)

- Monthly deduction and remittance for all employees
- PAYE returns must be filed by the 9th of the following month

### Withholding Tax

- Applicable to certain payments including professional fees, rent, and interest
- Must be remitted by the 20th of the following month

### Tax Compliance Certificate

- Required for government tenders, licensing, and regulatory approvals
- Must be renewed before expiry

## Employer Statutory Obligations

### NSSF (National Social Security Fund)

- Register as an employer
- Register all employees
- Monthly contribution calculation and remittance
- File monthly returns

### SHA (Social Health Authority)

- Register as an employer
- Enroll employees and dependants
- Monthly contribution calculation and remittance
- File monthly returns

### Housing Levy

- Monthly deduction and remittance as required by current legislation
- File monthly returns alongside PAYE

## Document Retention

Businesses should maintain organized records of:

- Certificate of Incorporation and CR12
- KRA PIN certificates
- Tax returns and payment receipts
- NSSF and SHA registration documents
- Monthly remittance receipts
- Business permits and licences
- Annual return filing receipts
- Director and shareholder records

## Compliance Calendar

A well-maintained compliance calendar should track:

- **Monthly**: PAYE, NSSF, SHA, VAT (if applicable), withholding tax
- **Quarterly**: As required by specific obligations
- **Annually**: BRS annual returns, income tax, business permit renewals
- **As needed**: TCC renewals, director change filings, address updates
