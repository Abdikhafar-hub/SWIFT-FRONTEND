## What Is a KRA PIN?

A KRA PIN (Personal Identification Number) is a unique identifier assigned by the Kenya Revenue Authority to individuals and entities for tax purposes. It is a fundamental requirement for engaging in most economic activities in Kenya.

## Who Needs a KRA PIN?

Almost everyone engaging in economic activity in Kenya needs a KRA PIN:

- **Employed individuals** — Required by employers for PAYE processing
- **Business owners** — Needed for business registration and tax compliance
- **Company directors** — Required for company incorporation
- **Property buyers and sellers** — Needed for land and property transactions
- **Importers and exporters** — Required for customs clearance
- **Government service applicants** — Many eCitizen services require a KRA PIN

## Documents Required

### For Individual Registration

1. National ID card or passport
2. Passport-size photograph
3. Valid email address
4. Active mobile phone number

### For Business Registration

1. Certificate of Incorporation or Business Name Certificate
2. Directors' national IDs or passports
3. Directors' individual KRA PINs
4. Company's registered address details

## Registration Process Through iTax

### Step 1: Access the iTax Portal

Navigate to the KRA iTax platform. If you are registering for the first time, you will need to create a new account.

### Step 2: Complete the Registration Form

Fill in the PIN registration form with accurate personal or business details. The information must match your identification documents exactly.

### Step 3: Upload Supporting Documents

Upload the required identification documents as specified by the registration form.

### Step 4: Submit and Receive Your PIN

Upon successful processing, your KRA PIN certificate is generated and can be downloaded from the iTax portal.

## Individual vs Business KRA PIN

It is important to understand the distinction:

- An **individual KRA PIN** is for personal tax obligations
- A **business KRA PIN** is a separate registration for the business entity
- Company directors need individual PINs first, then register for the business PIN
- Both types have different tax obligations and filing requirements

## Common Mistakes

- **Incorrect personal details** — Information must match your ID exactly
- **Duplicate registrations** — Each person should have only one KRA PIN
- **Not updating details** — Address and contact changes should be updated on iTax
- **Confusing individual and business PINs** — These are separate registrations with different purposes

## Tax Obligations After Registration

Once you have a KRA PIN, you have tax filing obligations:

- **Income Tax Returns** — Annual filing by June 30th for individuals
- **VAT Returns** — Monthly filing if registered for VAT
- **PAYE** — Monthly filing if you have employees
- **Withholding Tax** — As applicable to your business activities
