## Why Register a Company in Kenya?

Registering a company in Kenya establishes your business as a separate legal entity. A registered company can enter into contracts, own property, and provides limited liability protection for its shareholders. It is also a requirement for accessing many government services, opening business bank accounts, and building credibility with clients and partners.

## Types of Companies You Can Register

The Business Registration Service (BRS) in Kenya facilitates the registration of several types of business entities:

- **Private Limited Company** — The most common structure for small and medium businesses. Limited to a maximum of 50 shareholders.
- **Public Limited Company** — Suitable for larger businesses that may seek public investment.
- **Company Limited by Guarantee** — Typically used for non-profit organizations.
- **Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)** — A partnership structure with limited liability protection.
- **Foreign Company Branch** — For international companies establishing a presence in Kenya.

## Documents Required for Company Registration

Before starting the registration process, ensure you have the following documents ready:

1. **National ID or Passport** — For all proposed directors and shareholders
2. **KRA PIN Certificate** — Each director must have a valid KRA PIN
3. **Passport-size Photographs** — Recent photos for all directors
4. **Proposed Company Name** — At least three name options for the name search
5. **Registered Office Address** — A physical address in Kenya
6. **Memorandum and Articles of Association** — The company's constitutional documents
7. **Share Capital Details** — The proposed share structure and allocation

## The Company Registration Process

### Step 1: Name Search and Reservation

The first step is conducting a name search through the BRS platform on eCitizen. This confirms whether your proposed company name is available and not too similar to existing registered names. It is advisable to prepare multiple name options.

### Step 2: Prepare Incorporation Documents

Once the name is reserved, the incorporation documents must be prepared. These include the memorandum and articles of association, which define the company's purpose, governance structure, and shareholder rights.

### Step 3: Compile Director and Shareholder Information

Gather all identification documents, KRA PINs, and photographs for the proposed directors and shareholders. Ensure all information is consistent across documents.

### Step 4: Submit Through eCitizen

The completed application, along with all supporting documents, is submitted through the eCitizen portal. Government filing fees are paid at this stage.

### Step 5: BRS Review

The Business Registration Service reviews the application. This review period varies depending on the completeness of the application and current processing volumes.

### Step 6: Receive Certificate of Incorporation

Upon approval, BRS issues the Certificate of Incorporation and CR12 (company particulars extract). These are the official documents confirming your company's registration.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Choosing a name too similar to existing companies** — This will result in rejection during the name search
- **Inconsistent information** — Director details must match exactly across all submitted documents
- **Missing KRA PINs** — All directors must have valid KRA PINs before the application can proceed
- **Incorrect share capital declarations** — Ensure the share structure is clearly defined and agreed upon by all shareholders

## After Registration: Next Steps

Once your company is registered, there are several statutory obligations to address:

- **KRA Registration** — Register for a business KRA PIN and set up tax obligations (Income Tax, VAT if applicable, PAYE)
- **NSSF Registration** — Required if you will be hiring employees
- **SHA Registration** — Mandatory health insurance contributions for employees
- **Business Permits** — County-specific business permits may be required
- **Bank Account** — Open a corporate bank account using your Certificate of Incorporation and CR12
