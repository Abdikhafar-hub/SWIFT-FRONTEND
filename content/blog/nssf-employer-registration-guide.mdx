## What Is NSSF?

The National Social Security Fund (NSSF) is a statutory social security scheme in Kenya. It provides retirement benefits and other social security protections for workers. All employers are required to register with NSSF and make monthly contributions for their employees.

## Who Must Register?

Every employer in Kenya who engages workers is required to register with NSSF. This includes:

- Companies and businesses with employees
- Organizations and institutions with paid staff
- Individual employers (domestic workers, farm workers, etc.)

## Registration Requirements

To register as an employer with NSSF, you typically need:

1. Certificate of Incorporation or Business Name Certificate
2. KRA PIN certificate (business)
3. Directors' identification documents
4. Business address and contact details
5. Employee information for initial registration

## The Registration Process

### Step 1: Gather Documentation

Compile all required business registration documents and employee details.

### Step 2: Submit Application

Submit the employer registration application to NSSF with all supporting documents.

### Step 3: Receive Employer Number

NSSF issues an employer registration number upon approval. This number is used for all future transactions.

### Step 4: Register Employees

Register each employee under the employer's NSSF account with their personal details.

## Monthly Compliance Obligations

Once registered, employers have ongoing monthly obligations:

- **Calculate contributions** based on current statutory rates
- **Deduct employee contributions** from payroll
- **Add employer contributions** as mandated
- **Remit total contributions** by the statutory deadline each month
- **File monthly returns** with NSSF

## Penalties for Non-Compliance

Late or missed contributions may attract:

- Penalty charges on outstanding amounts
- Interest on late payments
- Potential legal action for persistent non-compliance
